2. About AB GalleryClean

AB GalleryClean is an Android application designed to help users review and manage photos and videos stored on their device.

Its features may include:
- detecting duplicate photos;
- identifying visually similar photos;
- finding screenshots;
- identifying potentially blurred photos;
- finding large videos;
- finding older downloaded media;
- previewing media;
- allowing users to select and delete media files;
- estimating recoverable storage space.

The analysis of photos and videos is performed locally on the user's device.

AB GalleryClean does not upload the user's photos or videos to AB Lab AI servers for analysis.

3. Permissions and access to photos and videos

AB GalleryClean may request permission to access photos and videos stored on the Android device.

This access is used only to provide the app's core functions, including scanning, categorizing, previewing, comparing and allowing the user to delete selected media.

The app does not automatically delete files without the user's action.

On supported Android versions, deletion may require confirmation through Android's system media controls.

4. Data processed locally on the device

To provide its features, AB GalleryClean may process locally information such as:
- media identifiers;
- file names where available;
- file size;
- media type;
- creation or modification date where available;
- image thumbnails or image data required for local analysis;
- cryptographic hashes used to detect exact duplicates;
- perceptual image hashes used to detect visually similar photos;
- image sharpness information used to identify potentially blurred photos.

This processing is performed locally on the device.

This media-analysis information is not sent to AB Lab AI servers.

5. Information stored locally

AB GalleryClean may store app preferences locally on the device, including:
- selected language;
- analysis thresholds and preferences;
- Free-plan daily deletion count;
- Premium entitlement cache;
- app configuration data;
- privacy/advertising state managed by Google SDKs where applicable.

Local preferences may be stored using Flutter and Android local storage mechanisms.
